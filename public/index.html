    Where do I find this?

    Canvas: Calendar → "Calendar Feed" at the bottom right → copy the link.
    Blackboard: Calendar → gear icon → "Share calendar" → copy the iCal link.
    Google Classroom: open the class calendar in Google Calendar → Settings → "Secret address in iCal format".
    Moodle: Calendar → "Export calendar" → "Get calendar URL".
