Get started

iPhones only show reminders from apps on your Home Screen, so add StuCal there first. It takes 10 seconds.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen , then Add.
  3. Open StuCal from your Home Screen to finish setting up.

Needs iOS 16.4 or newer. Using Chrome on iPhone? Open this page in Safari instead.

Where do I find this?

Canvas: Calendar → "Calendar Feed" at the bottom right → copy the link.
Blackboard: Calendar → gear icon → "Share calendar" → copy the iCal link.
Google Classroom: open the class calendar in Google Calendar → Settings → "Secret address in iCal format".
Moodle: Calendar → "Export calendar" → "Get calendar URL".
