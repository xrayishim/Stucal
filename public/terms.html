What StuCal is

StuCal is a free reminder service. It reads the school calendar link you give it (such as your Canvas calendar feed) and assignments you add yourself, and reminds you before things are due by phone notification or text message. StuCal is in beta, so features may change.

StuCal is a helper, not a guarantee

We work hard to send reminders on time, but notifications and texts can be late or not arrive (for example, if notifications are turned off on your phone), and your school's calendar can be wrong or incomplete (for example, if a teacher doesn't put an assignment on Canvas). You are still responsible for knowing your own deadlines. StuCal is not responsible for missed assignments, late penalties or grades.

Text message program

This section applies if you sign up with your phone number.

Program: StuCal assignment reminders.

What you'll get: recurring automated texts with login codes, reminders before your assignments are due, and (if turned on) a Sunday summary of your week.

How often: message frequency varies with your assignments, usually a few texts a week.

Cost: StuCal is free. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

Opting out: reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Reply START to turn them back on. Reply HELP for help, or contact us below.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Only sign up with a phone number that belongs to you.
